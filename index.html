Voice for your Mac and your coding agent

Say it. Your Mac does it.

Hold a button, knock twice or press your AirPods, and speak. Xpu Command types your words, hands them to Claude Code or Codex, or acts on your Mac. It asks before anything risky.

Four ways to start talking

Nothing listens until you ask it to.

There is no wake word and no always-on microphone. You start each request yourself, in whichever way your hands are free.

Side button

Hold and speak

Hold your mouse's forward button, say what you want, let go. It sends when you release.

⌃ ⌥ Space

From the keyboard

For trackpads. Hold the shortcut, or click once to start and once to send.

Knock knock

Tap the laptop twice

Two knocks on the palm rest, read from the MacBook's motion sensor. Taps while you type are ignored.

AirPods

Press once

One press on your AirPods starts listening. Double and triple press still skip tracks.

What it does with your words

Type it, code it, or just get it done.

Type where your cursor is

Dictation into any app: Slack, Mail, Cursor, ChatGPT or a terminal. Transcribed on your Mac by Whisper.

you“Tell Sara the build is green and I'll ship it tonight.”
typedTell Sara the build is green and I'll ship it tonight.

Send it to your coding agent

Claude Code or Codex works on your project folder. Every command is asked out loud, and the answer is read back to you.

you“Add a test for the login page.”
claudeEditing login.test.ts
asksRun the command: npm test?
you“Yes.”
doneAdded three tests for the login page. All pass.

Control your Mac

Open apps, play music, set reminders and events, draft emails, and work inside the apps you allow. Anything that changes your data waits 3 seconds so you can cancel.

you“Remind me to call Sara tomorrow at 10.”
about toReminder: Call Sara · Mon 16 Nov 10:00
doneCreated the reminder. Say “undo that” to take it back.

Built so you can let it act

You decide what it may touch.

  • Every app starts OffChoose See, Control or Ask for each app. Password managers, Keychain and System Settings are never allowed.
  • Approve by voiceSay yes, no or always. Deleting, pushing or running downloaded code needs a spoken yes.
  • Undo for 30 minutesApps it opened close, reminders and notes it made are deleted, volume and music go back.
  • Speech stays on your MacWhisper runs locally. The 148 MB model is downloaded once and checked against its published fingerprint.

It gets to know you

Remembers what matters. Learns what you repeat.

Speaks Persian too

Choose Persian and answer with «آره»، «نه» or «همیشه». Say «بس کن» to stop and «برگردون» to undo. The agent replies in the language you spoke.

Install

Ready in about two minutes.

  1. Download the DMGFrom the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Drag Xpu Command into ApplicationsOr open it and choose Move to Applications when it asks.
  3. Follow Get startedAllow Accessibility and the microphone, and download the speech model. Then hold the button and say hello.
