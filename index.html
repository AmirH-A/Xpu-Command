Get started

A few steps, once.

    Talk

    How you start and stop talking.

    More options

    The talk button stops doing its usual job while Xpu Command runs. Pause it from the menu-bar icon.

    Speech

    Turning your voice into text.

    DownloadWhisper, from Hugging Face (whisper.cpp). Checked against its published fingerprint.

    فارسی: Small (multilingual) works best. Say «آره»، «نه» or «همیشه» to answer, «بس کن» to stop, «برگردون» to undo. Persian answers are shown, not read aloud.

    OpenAI key

    Audio goes to OpenAI only while Cloud is on. The key stays in your keychain.
